Drishti privacy

Drishti privacy for mindful screen time.

This page explains what information Tiny Studio may receive through Drishti, how it is used, and the choices you have. Drishti is Tiny Studio’s mindful screen time app for interrupting the scroll reflex.

Effective date: March 14, 2026 App-specific policy URL

What this page covers

  • What information Tiny Studio may receive and how it is used.
  • Retention and the choices you have.

Advertising and tracking

No third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

The Drishti website has no public sign-in system and does not process payments. In-app accounts or payments are not described on this page.

Support information

What Tiny Studio may receive

Support emails may include information you choose to send, such as your message, screenshots, and device details that help explain an issue.

Use and retention

To respond, improve, and keep records when necessary.

Support information may be used to answer questions, troubleshoot issues, improve product quality, and keep reasonable records of support conversations.
