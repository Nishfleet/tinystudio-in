Drishti

Mindful pause before distraction

Drishti helps bring awareness to screen time before distraction takes over.

Get early access Drishti support Drishti privacy

Drishti is a mindful screen-time app built around intentional pauses. When you try to open a distracting app, it creates a moment to breathe, check your intention, and choose with more awareness instead of running on autopilot.
