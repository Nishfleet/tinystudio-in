Privacy choices

Privacy questions and data requests have a clear route.

Use this page for privacy-related questions about a Tiny Studio app, including requests involving access, correction, deletion, or another privacy concern.

Email a privacy request Studio privacy

What to include

  • The app name involved in the request
  • The type of request you are making
  • The email address connected to the app, if relevant
  • Enough context for Tiny Studio to understand the issue

Access

Ask what information may be involved.

If you need help understanding what information may have been shared through support or connected to your use of an app, this is the right place to start.

Correction or deletion

Use the same privacy inbox.

Correction and deletion requests can be sent through the privacy email path so they stay connected to the rest of the studio’s public support flow.

Current setup

The website itself is lightweight.

Tinystudio.in does not currently offer user account sign-in or payment processing, so privacy questions will usually relate to an app or a support email conversation.
