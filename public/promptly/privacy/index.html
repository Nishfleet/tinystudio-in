Promptly privacy

Promptly privacy for bookings, reminders, and client data.

This page explains what data Promptly handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Promptly is Tiny Studio’s booking and no-show prevention app for solo professionals.

Effective date: March 14, 2026 App-specific policy URL

What this page covers

  • What data Promptly handles and how it is used.
  • Retention, service providers, and your choices.
  • Privacy and support questions can be sent to Tiny Studio’s support inbox.

Advertising and tracking

No third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Promptly is a booking workflow for solo professionals. This policy does not describe targeted advertising, cross-app tracking, or selling personal information.

Professional data

Account, onboarding, service, and calendar details.

Promptly may process professional profile details, region, role, service settings, booking records, reminder preferences, calendar event details, phone OTP status, and notification settings.

Client and support data

Booking links, notes, payment proof, and support records.

Client names, contact details, booking actions, cancellation or reschedule links, professional notes, screenshots, payment-proof records, and support messages may be handled where the workflow requires them.

Use, retention, and service providers

Promptly may use this information to provide bookings, reminders, calendar clarity, client action links, payment-proof context, support, security, abuse prevention, and product maintenance. Records are kept only as long as needed for the app workflow, support, security, legal, or accounting reasons. Service providers may support hosting, storage, authentication or OTP delivery, notifications, calendar connectivity, support, and payment-proof handling. Promptly should not receive full payment-card numbers.

Your choices

  • Promptly support email
  • Privacy choices and requests
  • Calendar and notification access can be changed in device settings.
  • Clients can use signed booking links without installing the app.

Important note

If Promptly adds analytics, crash reporting, new payment processors, expanded account sync, or another external service not described here, this page will be updated to describe it.

Support information

Support emails may include information you choose to send, such as your message, screenshots, device details, app version, and the booking or payment-proof context needed to investigate an issue.
