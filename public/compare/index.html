Guide shape

Monthly retainers

The 2026 guides below list MSP marketing as recurring monthly retainers. 100Signals publishes that those retainers run $549 to $25,000 a month. Martal publishes that pricing spans roughly $500 to $20,000 per month, with full-service retainers typically starting near $3,000.

Guide shape

Audit packages

The same guides also describe audit-led entry: 100Signals notes JumpFactor project work and audit engagements alongside a $5,000-$15,000/mo retainer. Digital1010 says some MSP-agency pricing is scoped to the engagement and quoted after the audit. That is a diagnosis package, not a one-page correction sprint.
