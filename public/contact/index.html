What to send

For app support, include the product name, the device, the iOS version, and the step where the issue happened. For privacy requests, use the privacy links so the request lands with enough context to identify the right app surface.

What stays here

This contact page is intentionally simple: one studio inbox, direct product links, and clear routes to support, privacy, terms, Promptly, and Drishti. It should remain useful even before App Store badges and launch pages are added.

For app users

Promptly questions should mention bookings, reminders, payment proof, or client action links when relevant. Drishti questions should mention screen time, pause flows, permissions, or mindful tracking when relevant. That keeps the reply tied to the right product instead of forcing every request through a generic studio inbox.

For public requests

Privacy, support, and terms links stay visible from this page because those are the links app stores, reviewers, and early users need most often. New product pages can be added here only when they have matching support and privacy destinations.
