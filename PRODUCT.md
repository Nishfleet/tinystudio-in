# Product Context

TinyStudio Growth Brain is an agency operating system, not a SaaS product yet.

The first productized service is the Tangible Revenue Leak Sprint + Search Trust Layer. It uses the TinyStudio Growth Brain system to help ecommerce and founder-led businesses turn existing customer, competitor, website, ad, and email context into clearer money pages, cleaner service-page structure, and better marketing assets.

The service can expand into a Full-Stack Growth Desk, but only after proof exists. TinyStudio can cover SEO, paid search, paid social, email/SMS, content, social distribution, local/reputation, analytics, CRO, creative, and marketing automation through channel readiness gates.

## Users

- Nish, selling and delivering the first client sprints.
- Codex, acting as the build and delivery operator.
- Future contractors, who should be able to follow the checklists without guessing.
- Clients, who receive clear fixes, drafts, and weekly learning reports.

## Current Offer

The first offer is a paid sprint, not a retainer and not software:

- Diagnose the biggest conversion leaks.
- Fix buyer clarity before SEO recommendations.
- Add the Search Trust Layer: title/meta, headings, internal links, FAQ blocks, schema where useful, sitemap/canonical/crawl basics, and local/service-area relevance.
- Use off-site SEO only as real trust/distribution: citations, Google Business Profile, partner/vendor listings, reviews, proof assets, useful citeable pages, and real PR/community mentions.
- Build a simple client brain from real context.
- Run six repeatable agent workflows.
- Deliver rewritten page sections, ad angles, email/SMS drafts, competitor notes, and a 30-day action plan.

## Expansion Offer Ladder

- Proof Sprint: `$1,000-$5,000`, first visible improvement and proof packet.
- Weekly Growth Desk: `$2,000-$5,000/month`, one visible improvement loop per week.
- Full-Stack Growth Desk: `$5,000-$12,000/month`, multi-channel execution across ready channels.
- Operator-Led Growth Pod: `$12,000+/month`, high-LTV multi-channel clients after paid-client proof and delivery capacity exist.

Full-stack does not mean every channel every month. It means the right channel is selected, shipped, measured, and explained through the proof loop.

## Trust Wedge

TinyStudio has no earned agency trust yet, so the wedge is tangible improvement proof, not vague marketing claims. The job is to make every delivery inspectable enough that a skeptical buyer can see what improved without taking our word for it.

Every client sprint must make the delta inspectable:

- Before: the leak, confusion, missing proof, or unmeasured state.
- After: the specific page, copy, offer, workflow, or measurement improvement.
- Proof source: screenshot, shipped file, test result, analytics, client fact, or Loom.
- Client-visible value: why the change matters in plain business English.
- Next measurement: the next signal that proves whether the change worked.
- Measurement contract: the signal, source, owner, next check, baseline/current state, and decision rule.
- Retention signal: whether the client saw the value, understood the next action, and wants to continue.

Dashboards are only the surface. The moat is the weekly discipline of shipping, proving, explaining, and re-measuring visible improvements.

## Success Criteria

- The offer can be explained in one minute.
- The first client can buy without waiting for new software.
- Delivery can start from a client website, analytics screenshots, reviews, ads/emails, and competitor URLs.
- Every output has a human approval gate.
- Every page, form, ad, and email recommendation runs through the conversion optimization playbook before final delivery.
- Every page fix follows the order: conversion first, on-site search trust second, off-site trust/distribution only when real.
- Every expanded channel passes the channel readiness scorecard before it is sold or delivered.
- Every sprint shows one tangible before/after improvement with proof, value, measurement contract, and client confirmation.
- Every client leaves with a reusable client brain and a next-week measurement loop.
- No customer-facing claim is included unless it is true and provable.
