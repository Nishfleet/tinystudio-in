# Product context

TinyStudio currently sells one human-reviewed managed service: the **The 7-Day Website Correction**.

## Buyer

Founder-led Managed IT/MSP/cybersecurity companies with a live site and high-value offer. A fit has one page where buyer clarity, trust, or implementation readiness is the highest-leverage constraint.

## Pilot

The first 3 clients pay exactly **$1,000 founder pilot**. No alternate price or package is active for these first three clients.

## Fixed scope

One highest-leverage page receives:

- a leak map
- a rewrite or redesign
- one implementation pass **or** a dev-ready handoff
- search-trust basics
- before/after proof
- a Loom walkthrough
- a measurement plan
- one revision
- 14-day implementation tracking

Day 0 starts only after payment, required context, an approval owner, and an implementation owner. Client delay pauses the clock.

## Boundaries

TinyStudio makes no revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume guarantees. Human review gates fit, claims, client-facing work, delivery/acceptance, and renewal. Automation prepares research, drafts, QA, packages, and routing; it cannot autonomously send, publish, spend, approve, accept, or renew.

## SaaS graduation evidence gate

Graduation is allowed only with at least 10 paid sprints, the same problem in at least 7, at least 70% workflow repeatability, usefulness at least 8/10, approval at least 70%, recurring need, and at least 3 deposits or preorders. Until then, this is a managed service.

Threshold form: **>=10 paid sprints**, **same problem >=7**, **>=70% workflow repeatability**, **usefulness >=8/10**, **approval >=70%**, recurring need, and **>=3 deposits/preorders**.

`public/` remains the separate tinystudio.in portfolio and is excluded from service truth checks.
