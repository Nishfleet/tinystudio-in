# Rob Hoffman Agency Video Notes

Source: `https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2045911313520496640/vid/avc1/1080x1920/pPUqpncjtFJeqN7o.mp4?tag=21`

Date inspected: 2026-05-29

## Summary

The video argues that AI-enabled agencies have two opportunities:

- improve margins by systemizing repeated service work
- expand from one service into multiple related service lines while keeping a human management layer

The more important point for TinyStudio is the second one. A single-service agency is easier to replace. A company that manages a core marketing function with one relationship, one invoice, and a weekly operating loop becomes stickier.

## Takeaways For TinyStudio

- Start with one wedge, not a giant menu.
- Build a workflow around that wedge.
- Put a human approval layer on top.
- Expand into adjacent services only after the first workflow is repeatable.
- Position the retainer as a weekly outsourced growth desk, not just more assets.
- Use the client brain as the durable context layer across all service lines.

## Service Lines Mentioned Or Implied

- SEO
- Paid ads
- Email marketing
- Broader outsourced marketing operations
- Business-process-style management of a core function

## Practical Change To The Plan

The 7-Day Growth Brain Sprint stays the entry offer.

The retainer becomes the strategic expansion path:

- weekly performance analysis
- page/offer/site architecture improvements
- ad and email angle creation
- competitor and AI/search visibility watch
- client brain maintenance

This keeps TinyStudio from becoming a one-off copy shop.
