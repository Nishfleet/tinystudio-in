# Solo AI Agency Pre-Customer Parity

Source benchmark: the X article "How I Run an AI Agency Solo (No Employees, $40k MRR)" by Ronin, read in the Codex in-app browser on 2026-06-04.

## What Parity Means Here

TinyStudio cannot honestly claim parity with the article's customer count, revenue, margin, model spend, or retention proof yet. Those require real customers and paid delivery.

Pre-customer / pre-revenue parity means the business has the same operating shape ready before proof starts:

- a specific productized offer, not "we do whatever you need"
- a defined four-stage delivery workflow: intake, system production, human review, handoff loop
- reusable delivery workflows and skills that reduce repeated founder labor
- a visible buyer-safe feature/workflow view
- a lightweight acquisition path with scored prospects, Loom scripts, outbox, follow-up, and sales flow
- proof-led content and owned-product examples clearly labeled as not paid-client proof
- explicit limits for claims, publishing, sending, client approval, and measurement
- a model/task routing standard that assigns routine production, specialist work, utility work, and manual judgment deliberately
- a pre-revenue agent diagram parity layer: operator touchpoints, intake, route, parallel production lanes, specialist escalation, verifier QA, handoff, skills/library feedback, report-only background pool, and unit-economics tracking
- a unit-economics ledger that blocks margin or cost claims until real paid-client rows exist
- a 90-day path from first proof sprint to recurring Growth Desk

## Current TinyStudio Interpretation

TinyStudio's first offer is the Tangible Revenue Leak Sprint + Search Trust Layer. The recurring path is Weekly Growth Desk, then Full-Stack Growth Desk only after channel readiness and paid-client proof exist.

The article sells AI automation retainers. TinyStudio should not copy that category blindly. The TinyStudio version is a marketing-ops workflow business: find buyer-facing leaks, produce approved fixes, hand over proof-backed assets, and run a weekly learning loop.
