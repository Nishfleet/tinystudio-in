# TinyStudio 90-Day Solo Agency Rollout

Source benchmark: the solo AI agency article read in the Codex in-app browser on 2026-06-04.

This adapts the article's 90-day path to TinyStudio's category: Tangible Revenue Leak Sprint + Search Trust Layer, then Weekly Growth Desk. It does not claim $10k MRR, $40k MRR, a specific margin, or customer proof before those exist.

## North Star

By day 90, TinyStudio should have one repeatable sales-to-delivery loop:

- one productized offer
- one first vertical
- one proof-led acquisition motion
- one delivery workflow with human QA
- one weekly continuation loop
- one pre-revenue agent diagram parity gate
- one unit-economics ledger based on real work
- one proof library that separates owned-product proof from paid-client proof

## Days 1-30: Nail The Offer And First Proof Batch

Goal: make the offer specific enough to sell and complete the first external proof loop.

Start here: `docs/strategy/90-day-start-here.md`.

Required actions:

- Keep the first offer fixed: Tangible Revenue Leak Sprint + Search Trust Layer.
- Keep the first wedge narrow: Texas managed IT / cybersecurity until evidence says otherwise.
- Record the first five approved Loom audits.
- Send through contact forms or DMs while sender trust is dirty.
- Run market learning after sends before changing the list, hook, message, or channel.
- Fix sender trust only with real postal address and DKIM selector.
- Use owned-product proof only as delivery proof, not external demand proof.
- Keep the friction-triggered build backlog parked until the first proof batch or paid sprint exposes real friction.
- Keep `agent:parity` passing, but do not build live unattended workers before proof says that is the bottleneck.

Milestone:

- 5 real Looms recorded
- 5 real sends
- at least one reply or a documented learning from completed follow-up
- no public outcome claims beyond proof-safe offer language

## Days 31-60: Turn The First Sprint Into A System

Goal: close and deliver the first paid sprint, then capture the workflow.

Required actions:

- Convert a won prospect only after a real close package and won-stage note.
- Start delivery only after payment or written approval is explicit.
- Use the client brain, delivery cockpit, proof review, acceptance checklist, and weekly report.
- Track model/tool route, review time, rework, client-visible value, and next measurement.
- Save every reusable delivery step into the proof library or workflow system.
- Ask for permission before using any sanitized before/after proof publicly.

Milestone:

- 1 paid sprint closed or a clear reason why the offer/channel failed
- 1 sprint delivered to client-ready status
- 1 filled unit-economics row based on real delivery
- 1 accepted weekly report or handoff loop

## Days 61-90: Compound Into Weekly Growth Desk

Goal: keep solo scale by systemizing the parts that repeat and protecting human judgment.

Required actions:

- Offer Weekly Growth Desk only when the sprint reveals a real measurement loop.
- Run weekly client value loop with a concrete improvement, proof source, and next check.
- Add channels only when channel readiness passes.
- Route routine production through the workhorse path, high-risk work through specialist/manual review, and cleanup through utility tools.
- Keep client relationship, claim approval, and final recommendations human-owned.
- Update pricing, scope, and vertical only from reply/call/delivery evidence.

Milestone:

- 1 retained client or documented retention blocker
- repeatable workflow exported for the client
- proof library updated with actual customer learning
- first reliable cost/time baseline for delivery

## What Still Blocks Article-Level Proof

The following cannot be claimed until current evidence exists:

- $10k MRR
- $40k MRR
- 90% margin
- under-$300/month delivery cost
- 14-client solo capacity
- model quality being indistinguishable for TinyStudio work
- background agents running client delivery unattended
- client outcomes, savings, revenue lift, ROAS, rankings, or conversion lift

## Weekly Review Rule

Every week, answer:

- What moved in the funnel?
- What changed in delivery speed or quality?
- What proof can be shown without overclaiming?
- What repeated work became a reusable workflow?
- What still needs human judgment?
- What is the single bottleneck for the next seven days?

## Build-Later Rule

The later-build backlog lives at `docs/strategy/friction-triggered-build-backlog.md`.

Do not build cold email setup, an intake form, unattended background agents, a hard-wired model router, or public case-study pages just because the article mentions those concepts. Build only after real sales or delivery friction proves the item is the bottleneck.
