# Refero Reference Board

Created: 2026-05-03

## Purpose

The current preview factory is generating pages from internal templates. That is why the pages feel samey and the copy feels generic. This board changes the source of truth: every future preview should be assigned a proven design archetype based on real production references before any page is rendered.

## Research Coverage

Refero was queried across these angles:

- salon, spa and beauty SaaS landing pages
- appointment scheduling and booking pages
- premium wellness and beauty commerce pages
- fitness studio and wellness marketplace pages
- medical, dental and clinic trust pages
- local service, contact and professional-services pages
- similar-screen expansion from the strongest GlossGenius, Mangomint and ClassPass references

The strongest references are below. These are not for copying pixels. They define layout, rhythm, copy jobs, trust patterns and visual range.

## What The Best Pages Do

### Common Baseline

- One strong hero job, not a generic brand slogan.
- Clear CTA above the fold.
- Services grouped by visitor intent, not by internal category.
- Real or placeholder-proof zones are visually separate from facts.
- Repeated CTAs after trust, services and FAQ.
- Strong section rhythm: hero, trust, services, why choose, proof, FAQ, CTA.

### Better-Than-Baseline Moves

- Use a different hero structure per business: split, centered, mosaic, booking widget, dark editorial, resource/contact.
- Match copy to the user's decision: "book", "call", "compare", "request", "claim", "choose", "check".
- Use one memorable visual motif per page: circular photo badge, service chips, bento collage, booking card, stats strip, editorial product card.
- Make missing real photos explicit in the concept instead of filling with fake stock.
- Give each niche a different proof shape:
  - salons: lookbook/service cards
  - clinics: safety/process cards
  - fitness: photo mosaic/class cards
  - tutors/coaches: booking widget/how-it-works
  - professional services: problem/solution cards and contact form

## Copy Cure

Bad preview copy says:

- "Experience premium services"
- "Where beauty meets excellence"
- "Your destination for wellness"
- "Book your transformation today"

Good preview copy should do one of these jobs:

- Reduce choice friction: "Cuts, colour and smoothing services, grouped so new clients know what to ask for."
- Make contact obvious: "Call the Euston shop with the service you need. The page keeps the main options above the fold."
- Explain a considered appointment: "Skin treatments need a consultation-led path, not a menu full of unsupported promises."
- Sell locality: "A clearer first stop for clients comparing salons around QVB before booking."
- Sell proof readiness honestly: "The structure is ready for approved chair, treatment-room and finish photos before launch."

## Batch Variation Rule

For any batch of 20+ previews:

- No more than 15% should use the same archetype.
- No adjacent businesses in the same city should share the same hero structure.
- No more than two pages in a row can share the same palette family.
- Every page must have a unique copy job: booking clarity, portfolio proof, clinical trust, local urgency, natural/editorial, contact-first, pricing/menu clarity, or photo-led discovery.
- If approved images are missing, the archetype must use an abstract concept visual or honest "photo slots" instead of fake lifestyle photography.
