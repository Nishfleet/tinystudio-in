# Channel Readiness Scorecard

Use this before selling or delivering any channel beyond the first proof sprint.

## Rule

Do not sell a channel because the client asked for it. Sell it only when the client has the access, economics, signal, and approval cadence to make the channel worth running.

## Channel Gates

### CRO / Conversion

Must have:

- page or funnel to improve
- buyer action to measure
- proof source or customer context
- implementation path

Do not sell if the client only wants a prettier page.

### SEO / Search Trust

Must have:

- editable site
- clear buyer/search intent
- crawl/index basics available
- page structure or content gap worth fixing

Do not sell ranking promises, bought links, fake citations, doorway pages, or AI-only hacks.

### Content / Authority

Must have:

- real expertise or proof
- distribution plan
- internal linking plan
- measurement beyond word count

Do not sell generic blog volume.

### Paid Search

Must have:

- ad budget
- conversion tracking
- landing page
- offer clarity
- sales follow-up path

Do not manage spend without knowing the conversion economics.

### Paid Social

Must have:

- creative input
- audience hypothesis
- budget
- landing page
- refresh cadence

Do not sell paid social without creative testing.

### Email/SMS/Lifecycle

Must have:

- consented list
- ESP/SMS access
- deliverability basics
- segmentation or lifecycle moment
- unsubscribe/compliance path

Do not send to scraped or unconsented lists.

### Social / Distribution

Must have:

- founder/client voice
- posting approval cadence
- target audience
- reuse/distribution plan

Do not sell generic daily posting.

### Local/Reputation

Must have:

- real service area/location
- Google Business Profile or directory access
- review request process
- testimonials/proof assets

Do not create fake reviews, fake locations, or fake citations.

### Analytics / Attribution

Must have:

- source-of-truth metric
- analytics access
- conversion event definition
- reporting owner

Do not optimize channels without a measurement contract.

### Creative / Design

Must have:

- approved message
- proof source
- usage context
- approval path

Do not create creative that makes unsupported claims.

### Marketing Automation

Must have:

- clear recurring input
- clear action
- clear output
- clear verification signal
- human review gate

Do not automate publishing, sending, claim approval, or budget changes without explicit human approval.

## Retainer Fit

| Retainer | Minimum Channel Readiness |
|---|---|
| Weekly Growth Desk | CRO/search trust plus one of email, content, paid angle support, or reputation |
| Full-Stack Growth Desk | CRO, analytics, and at least two active growth channels |
| Operator-Led Growth Pod | CRO, analytics, paid or SEO engine, lifecycle/email, and implementation capacity |

## Output

Every channel decision must produce:

- chosen channel
- why now
- why not other channels
- access needed
- first improvement
- next measurement
- stop condition
