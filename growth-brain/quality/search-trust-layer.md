# Search Trust Layer

Use this after the revenue fault is understood and the conversion fix is clear.

## Rule

Conversion fixes come first. Search trust comes second. Off-site work is only for real trust and distribution.

Do not sell generic SEO, backlink packages, AI-search hacks, or ranking promises. Sell a clearer buyer page that search engines and AI-assisted search can also understand.

## Order Of Operations

1. Fix the money page so the buyer understands the offer, proof, objection answers, and next action.
2. Add the on-site search trust layer so Google, AI systems, and searchers can understand the page job.
3. Add off-site trust/distribution only when it reflects something real about the business.
4. Measure buyer action first, then organic discovery signals.

## On-Site Search Trust Checklist

Every fixed page should be checked for:

- page title and meta description
- one clear H1 and logical H2/H3 structure
- internal links to the next best service, proof, FAQ, pricing, booking, or contact page
- service-page structure with who it is for, problem, solution, proof, objections, FAQ, and CTA
- FAQ blocks based on real buyer questions
- schema only when the visible page facts support it
- sitemap, canonical, robots, crawl, and index basics
- local or service-area relevance when the business serves a geography
- non-commodity content, examples, proof, or methodology that a generic AI answer could not invent

## Off-Site Trust / Distribution Checklist

Allowed off-site work:

- real citations and reputable directories
- Google Business Profile improvements
- partner, vendor, marketplace, or association listings
- review and testimonial collection
- founder, team, client, or project proof assets
- useful pages people may naturally cite
- real PR, podcast, community, or local mentions

Blocked off-site work:

- buying or selling links for ranking purposes
- automated link creation
- excessive link exchanges
- fake citations, fake reviews, fake mentions, or spam directory submissions
- doorway pages built only to rank for city/service variations
- hidden text, keyword stuffing, or AI-only pages
- any promise of rankings, traffic, revenue, or AI answer visibility

Policy note: Google Search Central treats buying or selling links for ranking purposes, excessive link exchanges, and automated link creation as link spam. It also frames generative AI search optimization as normal SEO grounded in useful, crawlable, people-first pages.

Sources:

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

## Measurement

Track qualified actions first: CTA clicks, form starts, form completions, booked calls, replies, purchases, and sales-call fit.

Track search trust second: indexed pages, Search Console impressions/clicks/queries, local profile actions, referral traffic, branded search, citation/listing completion, review count/sentiment, and organic assisted conversions.
