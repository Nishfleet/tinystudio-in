# Customer Delight Playbook

The sprint should feel more useful than expected.

## Delight Principles

- Make the work implementation-ready.
- Show the client something specific they missed.
- Reduce their decision burden.
- Give them internal-shareable language.
- Make the next step obvious.

## Delight Add-Ons

Pick 1-2 per sprint:

- "Fix this today" quick-win checklist.
- Before/after section copy.
- Founder voice summary.
- Competitor positioning map.
- FAQ bank.
- Search trust punch list: title/meta, headings, internal links, FAQ/schema/crawl basics, and real off-site trust/distribution.
- Proof asset the client can reuse in sales, founder updates, or partner conversations.
- Internal Slack/email summary the client can forward.
- 3 homepage hero options.
- 5 customer-language hooks.
- 1-page decision memo for the founder.

## Do Not Delight With

- extra unsupported claims
- more pages than scoped
- vague strategy
- more complexity
- discounts after the price is agreed

## Client Handoff Note

"If you only do one thing this week, do this: [specific action]. It is the simplest fix with the highest expected leverage."

## Customer-Perceived Value Loop

Do not treat a delivery as retention-ready just because TinyStudio can explain it internally. The client must confirm four things:

- They saw the before/after delta.
- They understood why it matters.
- They approved the next action or test.
- They gave a continue/retain signal, even if it is only "continue this lane next week".

## Value Proof Score

Every retained client should stay at 8/10 or higher before we ask for renewal.

| Area | Points | Standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Visible before/after | 2 | The client can see what changed without trusting our explanation. |
| Proof source | 2 | Screenshot, page source, test result, analytics, client fact, or shipped file. |
| Client-visible value | 2 | Plain-English business value, not activity. |
| Next measurement | 1 | One next signal we will check. |
| Approved proof | 1 | Client-facing claims are approved in the proof ledger. |
| Retention pulse | 1 | What felt valuable, unclear, health score, and risk are filled. |
| Delight add-on | 1 | One useful extra that reduces decision burden. |

## Monthly Retention Bar

A client should not be asked to continue because we were busy. They should be asked to continue because the month produced:

- at least one visible conversion improvement
- at least one search trust improvement
- at least one proof or distribution asset
- a measurement contract with a next decision
- a client-confirmed value signal

If any of those are missing, the next week should repair the gap before pitching renewal.
