# One-Page Offer

## Headline

Tangible Revenue Leak Sprint + Search Trust Layer

## Subhead

TinyStudio fixes buyer clarity first, then adds the search trust layer so searchers, Google, and AI-assisted search can understand the offer.

## Best Fit

- B2B service companies with high-value leads.
- Local-service businesses with confusing service pages.
- Ecommerce brands with real traffic, reviews, and weak product pages.
- Founders who already have an offer but need sharper execution.

## What You Get

- Site or product-page leak map.
- Homepage/service-page hierarchy fix.
- Buying-page copy rewrites.
- Search trust cleanup: title/meta, headings, internal links, FAQs, useful schema, sitemap/canonical/crawl basics, and local/service-area relevance.
- Real off-site trust/distribution plan: citations, Google Business Profile, partner/vendor listings, reviews, proof assets, useful citeable pages, and real PR/community mentions.
- FAQ and trust-section recommendations.
- Competitor and AI/search visibility notes.

## Why It Works

Most teams publish more content before the existing site is clear. The sprint fixes the foundation first: what you sell, who it is for, why buyers should trust it, where each page fits, and what to improve next.
