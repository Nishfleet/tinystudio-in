# Value Calculator

Use this to decide whether the sprint price makes sense.

## Simple Math

```text
extra_customer_value = average_customer_value * gross_margin
payback_customers_needed = sprint_price / extra_customer_value
```

## Sales Use

If one extra customer can pay back the sprint, the sales call is easier.

Example language:

"If one additional qualified customer is worth more than the sprint, then the question is whether the current site is creating enough confusion to justify fixing it now."

## Next Step

If the math supports the sprint, send the buyer room and proposal. If the math is weak, do not push the sprint; either find a higher-value wedge or mark the lead as not fit.

## Fit Rules

Good fit:

- One extra customer can pay back the sprint.
- The site has obvious clarity or trust faults.
- The client can approve changes.

Bad fit:

- Sprint requires many extra customers to pay back.
- The business has no traffic or leads.
- The client cannot say what a customer is worth.
