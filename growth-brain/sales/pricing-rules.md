# Pricing Rules

## Default Pricing

- First proof clients: `$1,000`.
- Standard sprint: `$2,500-$5,000`.
- Weekly Growth Desk: `$2,000-$5,000/month`.
- Full-Stack Growth Desk: `$5,000-$12,000/month`.
- Operator-Led Growth Pod: `$12,000+/month`.

## Price Decision

Pick the price after the lead score and value calculator are complete.

## When To Charge More

Charge closer to `$5,000` when:

- one customer is worth `$10,000+`
- there are multiple high-value service lines
- the buyer wants more than 3 pages reviewed
- implementation support is included
- there are multiple stakeholders

Charge `$5,000-$12,000/month` when:

- the proof sprint has shown visible value
- the client has at least two ready channels
- analytics and source-of-truth metrics are available
- implementation access is not blocked
- the engagement includes recurring SEO, paid, email/SMS, content, reputation, analytics, or CRO work
- ad spend, contractor fees, and tool spend are clearly separate from the management fee

Charge `$12,000+/month` only when:

- customer value supports the spend
- paid-client proof exists
- multiple channels need active management
- implementation support or contractor coordination is included
- monthly review must be board/operator-ready

## When To Stay Low

Use founder pricing only when:

- the client can become proof
- the project can be delivered quickly
- the buyer can approve fast
- the niche is strategically useful

## When To Walk Away

Walk away when:

- the buyer wants guaranteed outcomes
- the buyer wants unlimited implementation
- the economics do not support the sprint
- the buyer cannot provide context
- the buyer is not the decision-maker
- the buyer wants "all channels" without access, tracking, budget, or approval cadence
- the buyer wants us to hide ad spend, buy links, fake reviews, fake citations, or guarantee rankings/revenue

## Next Step

Put the selected price in the buyer room and proposal. Do not negotiate against yourself before the buyer objects.
