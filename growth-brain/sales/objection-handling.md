# Objection Handling

## "Can you guarantee results?"

No. The sprint does not promise revenue, rankings, ROAS, or sales lift. It promises a sharper diagnosis, implementation-ready fixes, and a measurement plan.

## "Why not just write more SEO content?"

Because more pages on a confusing site can make the problem worse. First we clarify the money pages, service hierarchy, internal links, trust sections, and FAQs.

## "Why are you using AI?"

AI helps move faster across research, synthesis, and drafts. Human judgment stays in charge of positioning, claims, final copy, and what gets shipped.

## "Can you implement everything?"

For the sprint, the default deliverable is implementation-ready copy and structure. Implementation can be scoped separately after we know the platform and effort.

## "This is expensive."

If the site sells a high-value service or product, one clearer money page can be worth far more than the sprint. If the economics do not support that, the sprint is not a fit.

## "We already have an SEO/marketing person."

Good. This sprint can give them a focused architecture and copy package to implement faster. It is not meant to replace every marketer.

## "Send more info."

Send the buyer room: Loom, one-page offer, scope, price, timeline, and next step. Do not write a long custom essay unless they are a strong fit.
