# Sales Call Script

## Goal

Confirm fit, sell the sprint, and avoid free consulting.

## Call Flow

1. "What made the audit worth replying to?"
2. "What is the page or offer supposed to do for the business?"
3. "What happens if this stays unclear for another 90 days?"
4. "Who needs to approve changes to the site or copy?"
5. "Do you already have reviews, analytics, competitors, or campaign history we can use?"
6. "If I can turn this into implementation-ready fixes in 7 days, is that worth doing now?"

## Qualification

Good fit:

- clear offer
- decision-maker present
- enough business context
- site/page leak visible
- sprint price is reasonable relative to customer value

Bad fit:

- no live offer
- no approval owner
- wants guaranteed outcomes
- wants a full rebuild for sprint pricing
- cannot provide context

## Close

"The sprint is 7 days. I will build the client brain, map the leak, rewrite the priority sections, give you competitor/search visibility notes, and hand you a 30-day action plan. The first step is payment and intake."

## If They Need To Think

"That is fine. I will send a one-page scope. The decision is simple: if the leak is worth fixing this month, we should do the sprint. If not, no pressure."
