# Sales Call Script

## Goal

Confirm fit for **The Website Correction**, agree on the one highest-leverage page, and avoid free consulting.

The first 3 clients are exactly **$1,000 founder pilots**. The call does not approve claims, client-facing work, delivery, acceptance, or renewal.

## Call Flow

1. "What made the audit worth replying to?"
2. "Which one page matters most, and what is it supposed to help the right buyer do?"
3. "What happens if this stays unclear for another 90 days?"
4. "Who will approve claims, proof, copy, and the final client-facing work?"
5. "Who can implement the reviewed change, and what access or handoff format will they need?"
6. "What page evidence, analytics, reviews, and customer objections can you share?"
7. "If the sprint gives you one reviewed, implementation-ready page fix with 14-day tracking, is that worth doing now?"

## Qualification

Good fit:

- founder-led Managed IT, MSP, or cybersecurity company with a live site and high-value offer
- one highest-leverage page can be selected
- approval owner and implementation owner are named
- enough page and business context is available
- the $1,000 founder pilot is reasonable relative to customer value

Bad fit:

- no live site or high-value offer
- no approval or implementation owner
- wants guaranteed outcomes
- wants multiple pages or a full rebuild for sprint pricing
- cannot provide the required context

## Scope To State

The sprint covers one highest-leverage page and includes:

- fault map
- rewrite or redesign
- one implementation pass or dev-ready handoff
- search-trust basics
- before/after proof
- Loom
- measurement plan
- one client revision
- 14-day implementation tracking

Human review gates cover fit, claims, client-facing work, delivery and acceptance, and any renewal or continuation decision. Prepared material remains draft until the relevant human approval is recorded.

## Close

"The Website Correction is a $1,000 founder pilot for one highest-leverage page. You receive the reviewed page fix, implementation or handoff, proof, measurement plan, and 14-day tracking. Day 0 starts only after payment, required context, an approval owner, and an implementation owner. Client delay pauses the clock."

Do not guarantee revenue, rankings, ROAS, conversion lift, booked calls, or sales volume.

## If They Need To Think

"That is fine. I will send the one-page sprint scope for human review. If this page is worth fixing now, we can take the next step. If not, no pressure."
