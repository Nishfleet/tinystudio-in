# Build roadmap

## Build and improve now

Only work that helps sell, deliver, prove, or retain **The Website Correction** for one highest-leverage page:

- exact-fit intake and private qualification
- deterministic review queue and bounded agent-work packets
- evidence-backed fault map and one-page rewrite or redesign
- one implementation pass or dev-ready handoff
- search-trust, before/after proof, Loom, and measurement checks
- one-revision acceptance boundary
- 14-day implementation tracking and client-confirmed usefulness
- human gates for fit, claims, client-facing work, delivery/acceptance, and renewal

The first 3 clients receive the **$1,000 founder pilot**. Day 0 requires payment, context, approval owner, and implementation owner; client delay pauses the clock. No revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume guarantees.

## Do not build or activate

- a customer dashboard
- autonomous send, publish, spend, approval, acceptance, or renewal
- new packages or recurring offers
- provider integrations without a reviewed need
- software positioning before the evidence gate

The software graduation evidence gate remains blocked until at least 10 paid sprints, the same problem in at least 7, at least 70% workflow repeatability, usefulness at least 8/10, approval at least 70%, recurring need, and at least 3 deposits or preorders.
