# Warm Network Scripts

Warm intros are the fastest path to the first sprint.

## Referral Ask

Hey [Name],

I am opening 3 founder-priced slots for a 7-day website revenue leak sprint.

Best fit: B2B/local service businesses with a live site, high-value customers, and confusing service pages.

I record a short audit first, so there is no vague pitch.

Do you know 1-2 founders/operators whose website is probably leaving clarity or leads on the table?

## Existing Founder DM

Hey [Name], I am testing a new TinyStudio offer: a 7-day sprint where I find the biggest website clarity leaks and turn them into implementation-ready page fixes.

If you send me a site, I will tell you quickly whether it is a fit. No pressure if not.

## After Referral

Hey [Name], [Referrer] suggested I look at your site.

I help B2B/local service companies fix confusing pages before they spend more on content or ads.

If you are open to it, I can record a short audit showing the first leak I would fix.

## Rule

Ask for referrals before cold outreach every day.
