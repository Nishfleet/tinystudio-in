# Vertical Playbook: Managed IT And Cybersecurity

## Why This Vertical

- High-value B2B leads.
- Complex services that often blur together.
- Buyers need trust before contacting sales.
- Search intent is usually valuable and specific.

## Common Site Leaks

- Homepage tries to sell managed IT, cybersecurity, compliance, cloud, and helpdesk equally.
- Service pages are thin or duplicated.
- Headings do not map to buying intent.
- Trust proof is vague.
- Compliance expertise is buried.
- FAQs do not answer risk, support, response time, or audit questions.

## Loom Hook

"Your site is asking buyers to understand five service lines at once. I would make the homepage own the primary buying intent, then give each high-value service its own page and proof path."

## Pages To Check

- Homepage
- Managed IT services
- Cybersecurity services
- Compliance service pages
- Industries served
- Case studies/testimonials
- FAQ

## Proof To Look For

- certifications
- partner badges
- response time claims
- regulated industries served
- client logos or testimonials
- case studies
- local presence

## High-Intent Questions

- Who handles my IT support?
- How fast do you respond?
- Do you support compliance audits?
- What security frameworks do you know?
- Do you work with companies like ours?

## First Fix To Suggest

Clarify the homepage around the main money service, then create a service hierarchy that links to dedicated managed IT, cybersecurity, and compliance pages.
