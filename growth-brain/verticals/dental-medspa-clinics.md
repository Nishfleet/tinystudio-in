# Vertical Playbook: Dental, Med Spa, And Clinics

## Why This Vertical

- Strong local intent.
- Trust, proof, and visuals matter.
- Service pages often lack patient decision support.
- One new patient can justify the sprint for high-value services.

## Common Site Leaks

- Services are listed without explaining who each is for.
- Before/after proof is missing, weak, or not approved for use.
- Location trust is thin.
- CTAs are inconsistent.
- FAQs do not answer fear, cost, timing, recovery, or consultation questions.

## Loom Hook

"Patients are trying to decide whether they trust you before they contact you. The page needs to answer fear, proof, cost, timing, and next-step questions faster."

## Pages To Check

- Homepage
- Main service page
- Location page
- About/provider page
- Reviews
- Financing/pricing
- FAQ

## Proof To Look For

- provider credentials
- patient reviews
- before/after assets, if approved
- local ratings
- treatment photos
- financing information
- consultation process

## High-Intent Questions

- Is this right for me?
- Who performs the treatment?
- What does it cost?
- How long does it take?
- What happens at the consultation?

## First Fix To Suggest

Rewrite the main service page as a patient decision page with proof, process, FAQs, and one clear booking CTA.
