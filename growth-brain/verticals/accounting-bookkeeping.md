# Vertical Playbook: Accounting And Bookkeeping

## Why This Vertical

- Clear local and niche search intent.
- Trust matters heavily.
- Many firms have generic pages that do not explain fit.
- Clients can justify the sprint if one new recurring client is valuable.

## Common Site Faults

- Homepage says "accounting services" without buyer specificity.
- Bookkeeping, tax, payroll, CFO, and advisory services are mixed together.
- No clear niche focus.
- Weak trust and credential sections.
- FAQ does not answer pricing, timing, software, or handoff concerns.

## Loom Hook

"Your site lists services, but it does not make it obvious which buyer should choose you and what problem you solve first."

## Pages To Check

- Homepage
- Services
- Bookkeeping
- Tax
- Payroll
- Advisory/CFO
- About
- Reviews/testimonials

## Proof To Look For

- credentials
- industries served
- software expertise
- client retention
- review language
- local proof

## High-Intent Questions

- Do you work with businesses like mine?
- What software do you support?
- How does onboarding work?
- How much cleanup is needed?
- What do you handle monthly?

## First Fix To Suggest

Create a clear homepage promise for the best-fit buyer and split services into pages that match the buyer's decision path.
