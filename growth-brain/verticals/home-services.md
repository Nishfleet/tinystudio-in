# Vertical Playbook: High-Value Home Services

## Why This Vertical

- Local search intent is strong.
- Reviews and trust drive calls.
- Many sites hide service areas, proof, and emergency details.
- One job can be high value.

## Common Site Leaks

- Homepage does not clearly state service area or core job type.
- Service pages are generic.
- Reviews are not tied to specific services.
- Emergency/availability details are unclear.
- Internal links do not connect location and service pages.

## Loom Hook

"A homeowner needs to know three things fast: do you handle my problem, do you serve my area, and can I trust you?"

## Pages To Check

- Homepage
- Main service pages
- Location/service-area pages
- Reviews
- Emergency page
- Financing/warranty pages
- FAQ

## Proof To Look For

- licenses
- insurance
- warranty
- reviews
- before/after photos
- local service areas
- emergency availability

## High-Intent Questions

- Do you serve my area?
- Can you come quickly?
- Are you licensed and insured?
- What does it usually cost?
- What warranty do you offer?

## First Fix To Suggest

Clarify the service-area plus main service path, then add trust proof and FAQs near the CTA.
