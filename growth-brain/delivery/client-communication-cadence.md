# Client Communication Cadence

## During Sprint

Before Day 1:

- Run `npm run client:kickoff -- clients/client-slug`.
- Send `kickoff-message.md`.

Day 1:

- Confirm intake received.
- Send top 3 leak hypotheses.

Day 3:

- Send draft direction.
- Ask for claim/proof approvals.

Day 6:

- Send handoff Loom.
- Confirm any blockers.

Day 7:

- Send final report and next 30-day plan.

## Response Standards

- Reply to client messages within 1 business day.
- Keep updates short and specific.
- Ask for approval in numbered bullets.
- Never bury the next action.

## Approval Message Format

"I need approval on these before I finalize:

1. [Claim]
2. [Proof point]
3. [Policy/pricing/detail]

Reply approved, edit, or remove for each."

## Weekly Growth Desk

Every Friday:

- what shipped
- what changed
- what we learned
- what is next
- what the client needs to approve
