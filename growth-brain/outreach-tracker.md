- `sent`

## Daily Minimum

- Find 20 businesses.
- Pick 5 strong fits.
- Record 3 Loom audits.
- Send 3 personal messages.
- Follow up with anyone who opened or replied.

## Fit Filter

Only record a Loom if the business has at least three of these:

- Live offer
- Visible traffic signal or active ads/social
- Real reviews
- Clear product catalog or service page
- Founder or operator contact
- Obvious page, offer, email, or ad fault
- Confusing homepage/service-page hierarchy
