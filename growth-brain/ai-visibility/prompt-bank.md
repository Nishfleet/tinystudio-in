# AI And Search Visibility Prompt Bank

Use these prompts to understand how buyers might research the client category in AI tools and search.

## Category Prompts

- "What should I look for when hiring a [category] in [location]?"
- "Best [category] for [buyer type]"
- "[category] vs [adjacent category]"
- "How much does [service] cost?"
- "Questions to ask before hiring a [category]"
- "Top risks when choosing a [category]"
- "What does a good [service] provider include?"

## Comparison Prompts

- "Compare [client] and [competitor]"
- "Alternatives to [competitor]"
- "Best [service] companies for [niche]"
- "Which [service] provider is best for [buyer type]?"

## Buyer-Intent Prompts

- "I need help with [pain]. What kind of company should I hire?"
- "How do I know if my business needs [service]?"
- "What should a [service] provider do in the first 30 days?"
- "What proof should I ask for from a [service] provider?"

## Output To Capture

- phrases repeated across answers
- missing proof or trust points
- competitors mentioned
- questions buyers ask before purchase
- claims the client should avoid
- content gaps that belong on money pages or FAQs

## Rule

Do not claim that optimizing these prompts guarantees AI visibility. Use them to discover buyer language, questions, and proof expectations.
