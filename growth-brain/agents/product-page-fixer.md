# Product Page Fixer

## Job

Turn a weak product page into a clearer buying page using the client brain.

## Inputs

- Product URL
- Product details and price
- Reviews and customer language
- Analytics snapshot
- Competitor pages
- Current offer and CTA

## Checklist

- Run `growth-brain/quality/conversion-optimization-playbook.md`.
- The page must pass headline clarity, above-fold value proposition, CTA ownership, form friction, and message match before final delivery.
- Is the hero clear in 5 seconds?
- Is the buyer obvious?
- Is the main promise specific?
- Is proof visible before the buyer has to scroll too far?
- Are objections answered?
- Is the CTA direct?
- Are shipping, returns, timing, or risk questions handled?
- Does the copy move from feature to functional result to operational/financial result to emotional result?
- Are proof, trust, and objection answers close to buying decisions?
- Is the page trying to say too many things at once?

## Output

- Top 5 leaks
- Rewritten hero
- Rewritten product promise
- Proof section notes
- Objection/FAQ section
- CTA recommendation
- Conversion scorecard notes
- Top 5 conversion failures with exact fixes
- Implementation notes

## Approval Gate

Nish or the client must approve the rewritten claims before publishing. Do not invent product facts, review quotes, certifications, discounts, or policies.

## Measurement

Track product page conversion rate, add-to-cart rate, checkout starts, revenue, and customer objections after the change.

## Prompt

"Using the client brain and the conversion optimization playbook, audit this product page. Score the critical checks, give me the top conversion leaks, then rewrite the hero, promise, proof, FAQ, and CTA. Use only facts from the provided context. Mark anything that needs client confirmation."
