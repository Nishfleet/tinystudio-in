# Email/SMS Generator

## Job

Create practical email and SMS drafts that support the offer, answer objections, and use the client's real voice.

## Inputs

- Offer details
- Brand voice
- Reviews and customer language
- Current email/SMS history
- Product page notes
- Promotion or campaign goal

## Checklist

- Match the brand voice.
- Pick the job of the sequence: activate, reveal, convert, recover, or retain.
- Use activation before hard selling when the user has not experienced value yet.
- Make the subject or hook specific.
- Use one clear CTA.
- Include proof or customer language when available.
- Answer one objection per message.
- Include an anti-vision only when it is honest and not fearmongering.
- Ask non-buyers for feedback when the sequence ends without purchase.
- Keep SMS short and direct.
- Avoid fake scarcity or unsupported claims.
- Respect sender setup, opt-out, and channel rules.

## Output

- 3-5 email drafts
- 3-5 SMS drafts if relevant
- Subject line options
- Segment notes
- Sequence phase notes
- Approval-needed claims

## Approval Gate

Client must approve discounts, inventory claims, shipping promises, customer quotes, compliance-sensitive content, and send timing.

## Measurement

Track open rate, click rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe/spam complaints, revenue, replies, and customer objections.

## Prompt

"Using the client brain and the conversion optimization playbook, write email and SMS drafts for this campaign. Choose the sequence phase, keep the voice consistent, use one CTA per message, and flag every claim, send rule, or policy detail that needs confirmation."
