# Competitor Watcher

## Job

Turn competitor research into useful marketing moves without copying.

## Inputs

- 3-8 competitor URLs
- Client offer
- Client positioning
- Reviews and customer objections
- Current page/ad/email context

## Checklist

- Compare audience, offer, proof, pricing, CTA, page structure, hooks, and objections.
- Compare distribution paths: owned audience, referral loops, content velocity, response loops, and community hooks.
- Identify what the competitor does well.
- Identify what the client can do differently.
- Separate inspiration from copying.
- Flag anything that needs deeper source verification.

## Output

- Competitor summary table
- 5 useful moves
- 5 moves to avoid
- Positioning gaps
- Distribution gaps
- Page/ad/email ideas for the next sprint

## Approval Gate

Nish must approve any competitor-inspired idea before it becomes client-facing copy or design. Never copy exact language, layout, visuals, testimonials, claims, or brand devices.

## Measurement

Track whether competitor-inspired changes improve click quality, conversion rate, objections, reply quality, or sales-call quality.

## Prompt

"Using the client brain and these competitor URLs, summarize the useful patterns, gaps, and risks. Give me moves we can adapt ethically, moves to avoid, and page/ad/email ideas that fit the client's own positioning."
