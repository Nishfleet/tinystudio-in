# Landing Page Fixer

## Job

Turn a vague landing page into a focused page with a clear audience, offer, proof, and next action.

## Inputs

- Landing page URL
- Target buyer
- Offer details
- Reviews or proof
- Analytics snapshot
- Competitor pages
- Mobbin/reference packet if visual direction is needed

## Checklist

- Run `growth-brain/quality/conversion-optimization-playbook.md`.
- The page must pass headline clarity, above-fold value proposition, CTA ownership, form friction, and message match before final delivery.
- Does the page say who it is for?
- Does the headline name the offer or category plainly?
- Does the page explain the problem, promise, proof, and process?
- Is there one primary CTA?
- Are objections handled before the CTA repeats?
- Does the page structure match the buyer's decision stage?
- Does the copy follow the direct-response flow: headline, problem, agitate, credibility, solution, proof, CTA?
- Does the So-What chain turn features into functional, operational/financial, and emotional outcomes?
- Are claims provable?

## Output

- Page diagnosis
- Revised section order
- Section-by-section copy
- Proof placement notes
- CTA notes
- Conversion scorecard notes
- Top 5 conversion failures with exact fixes
- Visual/reference notes if needed

## Approval Gate

Client approval is required for claims, customer examples, before/after language, pricing, legal-sensitive statements, and any proof assets.

## Measurement

Track page conversion rate, CTA clicks, form starts, purchases, booked calls, scroll depth if available, and reply quality.

## Prompt

"Using the client brain and the conversion optimization playbook, rebuild this landing page structure. Keep it focused on one buyer and one offer. Score the critical checks, rank the top 5 conversion failures, draft section copy, show why each section exists, and flag any claim that needs proof."
