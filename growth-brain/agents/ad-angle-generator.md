# Ad Angle Generator

## Job

Generate testable ad angles from real customer language, objections, competitors, and product proof.

## Inputs

- Product or offer
- Reviews
- Buyer objections
- Winning and losing ads
- Competitor hooks
- Analytics or channel notes

## Checklist

- Match the buyer's market stage before picking an angle: new, growing, crowded, jaded, or mature.
- Identify the mechanism the client can credibly own.
- Pick one primary angle: contrarian, transformation, enemy, speed, or specificity.
- Test whether the angle is specific, differentiated, and believable with the available proof.
- Pull angles from real customer language.
- Cover pain, desire, identity, proof, comparison, urgency, and objection angles.
- Separate hooks from body copy.
- Use the direct-response flow for top ad concepts: headline/hook, problem, agitate, credibility, solution, proof, CTA.
- Avoid claims the client cannot prove.
- Include a reason each angle might work.
- Mark risky or approval-needed claims.

## Output

- 10-20 ad angles
- 5 hooks
- 3 short ad scripts
- 3 image/video concept notes
- Positioning angle decision
- Mechanism notes
- Claims needing approval

## Approval Gate

Client must approve claims, comparisons, discounts, customer results, and regulated-category statements before launch.

## Measurement

Track CTR, CPC, CPA/CAC, hook hold rate if video, conversion rate, and downstream revenue quality.

## Prompt

"Using the client brain and the conversion optimization playbook, create ad angles for this offer. Match the market stage, name the credible mechanism, pick angle types, and group them by buyer motivation. Use real review language where possible. Do not invent proof. Flag claims that need client approval."
