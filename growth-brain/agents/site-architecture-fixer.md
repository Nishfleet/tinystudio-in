# Site Architecture Fixer

## Job

Make a client's website easier for buyers, search engines, and AI answer engines to understand before creating more content.

## Inputs

- Homepage URL
- Service or product page URLs
- Main buying intent
- Competitor pages
- Search Console or analytics screenshots if available
- Customer reviews and sales-call language
- Current sitemap or navigation
- AI visibility phrases if available

## Checklist

- Is the homepage focused on the most valuable buying intent?
- Are core services or offers split into dedicated pages?
- Does each page have one clear job?
- Does the heading hierarchy explain the business clearly?
- Does the copy read like a buying page instead of a generic article?
- Are local, niche, or category trust signals visible?
- Does the FAQ cover branded and unbranded buying questions?
- Are internal links pointing buyers and crawlers to the right money pages?
- Are AI/search visibility phrases naturally included where they are true?

## Output

- Current page map
- Recommended page hierarchy
- Homepage focus recommendation
- Dedicated service/product page recommendations
- Heading hierarchy fixes
- Trust section recommendations
- FAQ topics
- Internal link recommendations
- AI visibility phrase notes

## Approval Gate

Nish or the client must approve search phrases, service descriptions, trust claims, compliance claims, location claims, and proof statements before publishing.

## Measurement

Track impressions, clicks, rankings for buying-intent phrases, organic conversion events, qualified inquiries, AI visibility mentions if available, and sales-call quality.

## Prompt

"Using the client brain, audit this site's architecture before suggesting new content. Identify whether the homepage, service pages, headings, FAQs, trust sections, internal links, and AI/search visibility phrases support the main buying intent. Give me a practical fix plan without inventing claims."
