# Loom audit workflow

## Goal

Show one founder-led Managed IT/MSP/cybersecurity company one useful, evidence-backed page fix. The Loom supports the human-reviewed **The Website Correction**; it is not an automated diagnosis or outcome promise.

## Prepare

- Confirm a live site, high-value offer, named founder/operator, and one highest-leverage page.
- Use public, attributable evidence only.
- Record one observed leak, one buyer-impact hypothesis, one first fix, and one way to measure it.
- Run the local readiness and claims checks.
- Stop if a credential, authenticated console, private enrichment, or unsupported claim would be required.

## Record

1. Why this company and page fit.
2. The observed leak.
3. Why it may matter to the target buyer.
4. The first page fix.
5. The proof or measurement needed.
6. The exact sprint invitation.

The invitation states: first 3 clients at the **$1,000 founder pilot**; one highest-leverage page; leak map, rewrite or redesign, one implementation pass or dev-ready handoff, search-trust basics, before/after proof, Loom, measurement plan, one revision, and 14-day implementation tracking. Day 0 requires payment, context, approval owner, and implementation owner; client delay pauses the clock. No revenue, ranking, ROAS, conversion, booked-call, or sales-volume guarantees.

## Review and route

Automation may prepare the packet, recording brief, and message. A human reviews the evidence, claims, and client-facing material. The operator then chooses whether to send. The workflow never sends, publishes, spends, approves, accepts, or renews autonomously.
