# Retired: Full-Stack Growth Desk Workflow

> **Status: Retired historical reference.** This broad recurring-service workflow is not an active TinyStudio offer or delivery path. Use `human-review-service-engine.md` and `client-sprint-workflow.md` instead.

Use this when a client wants help across SEO, paid ads, email, content, social, analytics, CRO, reputation, or automation.

## Positioning

We are not a vague "do everything" agency.

We run a weekly proof loop across the channels that are actually ready:

1. find the highest-value leak
2. choose the right channel
3. ship one improvement
4. show proof
5. measure the next signal
6. decide whether to continue, expand, or stop

## Intake

Collect only what changes channel choice:

- business model and offer
- high-value conversion action
- average customer value or lead value
- current traffic and sales channels
- analytics/source-of-truth metric
- CRM, email/SMS, ad account, website, and Google Business Profile access
- reviews/testimonials/proof assets
- sales follow-up process
- content/brand voice approval owner
- monthly budget and implementation capacity

## Channel Selection Order

Always start with the channel that can show a visible improvement fastest without fake promises.

1. CRO / conversion
2. Analytics / attribution
3. Search trust / SEO basics
4. Email/SMS/lifecycle
5. Local/reputation
6. Paid search / paid social
7. Content / authority
8. Social / distribution
9. Creative / design
10. Marketing automation

## Weekly Loop

Each week must include:

- one shipped or implementation-ready improvement
- one measurement contract
- one proof source
- one client-visible value explanation
- one next action
- one stop/continue/expand decision

## Monthly Review

The monthly review must answer:

- What changed this month?
- What proof did the client see?
- Which channel produced the clearest signal?
- Which channel should stop or pause?
- Which channel deserves more budget?
- What is the next 30-day thesis?

## Expansion Rules

Expand from `$2,000-$5,000/month` to `$5,000-$12,000/month` only when:

- weekly proof loop is working
- the client has at least two ready channels
- analytics and attribution are clear enough
- implementation bottlenecks are not blocking value
- the next month has a concrete channel plan

Expand above `$12,000+/month` only when:

- paid-client proof exists
- customer value supports the spend
- implementation or contractor capacity exists
- the client needs a true multi-channel operator pod

## Channel Workflows

### SEO / Search Trust

- technical basics
- page title/meta
- headings and internal links
- service/page hierarchy
- FAQ/schema where useful
- crawl/canonical/sitemap basics
- local/service-area relevance
- proof-backed content opportunities

### Paid Ads

- offer and landing-page review
- tracking check
- keyword/audience hypothesis
- ad angle matrix
- creative brief
- budget and stop-loss rule
- weekly learning review

### Email/SMS

- consent and deliverability check
- welcome/activation sequence
- nurture sequence
- abandoned cart or lead recovery
- winback/reactivation
- review/testimonial request
- segmentation and measurement

### Content / Authority

- buyer questions
- expert POV
- proof-backed content
- comparison pages
- case studies
- distribution plan
- internal links and measurement

### Social / Distribution

- founder/client voice
- proof-backed post ideas
- distribution calendar
- repurposing plan
- community/partner channels
- engagement and referral signals

### Local / Reputation

- Google Business Profile
- directories/citations
- review request flow
- testimonials
- local/service pages
- partner/vendor listings
- reputation proof loop

### Analytics / Attribution

- source-of-truth dashboard
- conversion events
- UTMs
- lead/source reporting
- sales follow-up feedback
- weekly decision rules

## Guardrails

- No revenue, ranking, ROAS, or sales guarantees.
- No bought backlinks, fake reviews, fake citations, fake mentions, spam directories, doorway pages, hidden text, keyword stuffing, or AI-only ranking hacks.
- No ad spend management without tracking, budget, and stop rules.
- No email/SMS without consent and unsubscribe path.
- No client-facing claim without proof.
- No renewal pitch without shipped work, learning, next action, and client-confirmed value.
