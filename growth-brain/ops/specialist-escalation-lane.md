# Specialist Escalation Lane

## Purpose

The diagram has a specialist lane for expensive or high-risk work. TinyStudio needs the same lane before revenue so the operator does not treat every AI output as equal.

## Escalate When

Escalate from workhorse to specialist or manual when the task touches:

- client private data
- legal, compliance, regulated categories, or policy-sensitive copy
- pricing, guarantees, discounts, refunds, or payment links
- security, authentication, DNS, email sender setup, production access, or data deletion
- paid media budget, campaign changes, or tracking changes
- unsupported revenue, ROAS, ranking, conversion, savings, or retention claims
- unfamiliar integrations
- final client-facing recommendations

## Specialist Packet

Every escalation packet must include:

- original task
- source evidence
- exact risky claim or decision
- failure cost if wrong
- proposed answer
- safer fallback
- required human approval

## Specialist Outputs

Specialist output is allowed to:

- explain risk
- recommend safer wording
- define a manual verification step
- block the output
- route the work back to production with constraints

Specialist output is not allowed to:

- approve its own claim
- publish or send
- treat owned-product proof as paid-client proof
- create revenue or margin claims without real ledger rows
