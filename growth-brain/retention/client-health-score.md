# Retired: Client Health Score

> **Status: Retired historical reference.** This retained-client scoring routine is not part of the The 7-Day Website Correction. Use human-reviewed 14-day implementation tracking and a separate human continuation decision instead.

Use this weekly for retained clients.

## Score

| Signal | Score |
|---|---:|
| Client replies within 2 business days | 0-2 |
| Client approves or ships recommendations | 0-2 |
| Metrics are available | 0-2 |
| Weekly report is read or discussed | 0-2 |
| There is a clear next action | 0-2 |

## Value Multipliers

Add these notes beside the score:

- Conversion improvement shipped or approved:
- Search trust improvement shipped or approved:
- Proof/distribution asset created:
- Client-confirmed value signal:
- Monthly retention reason:

## Interpretation

- `8-10`: healthy
- `5-7`: watch
- `0-4`: churn risk

## Churn Risk Actions

If score is 5-7:

- simplify next action
- ask what feels least useful
- reduce scope to one lane

If score is 0-4:

- schedule a reset call
- show shipped work and value delivered
- ask whether the business priority changed
- propose pause or narrower scope if needed

Do not wait until renewal week to find out the client is not feeling value. Ask weekly what felt valuable, what felt unclear, and what would make next week obviously worth it.
