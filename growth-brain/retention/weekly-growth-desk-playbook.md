# Weekly Growth Desk Playbook

## Positioning

The retainer is not "more deliverables". It is a weekly growth operating rhythm.

## Weekly Deliverable

Every week includes:

- performance review
- one priority decision
- one shipped or implementation-ready conversion improvement
- search trust review: title/meta, headings, internal links, FAQ/schema/crawl basics, and real off-site trust/distribution
- client brain update
- next-week recommendation
- one retention value stack: most valuable change, quick win, client ask, and reason to continue

## Weekly Retention Product

Every weekly update should make the client feel four things:

1. "They found a real leak I can see."
2. "They shipped or handed off the highest-leverage next fix."
3. "They showed me proof and a measurement plan."
4. "I know exactly why continuing next week is useful."

If a week cannot show those four things, reduce scope until it can.

## Automatic Checkups

Run this before weekly client updates:

```bash
npm run retention:checkups
```

Run this during the final week of the month:

```bash
npm run retention:checkups -- --monthly
```

Outputs:

- `growth-brain/ops/retention-checkups.md`
- `growth-brain/ops/retention-dashboard.html`

The checkup prepares the review. It does not send messages automatically.

The active Codex automation `TinyStudio Retention Checkups` runs this prep every Friday and flags month-end reviews. Treat the output as the owner work queue, not as an auto-send system.

## Monthly Rhythm

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Review metrics and choose the monthly lane |
| 2 | Ship first conversion improvement and search trust cleanup |
| 3 | Ship second improvement, proof asset, or real distribution move |
| 4 | Report, learnings, next-month plan, and retention confirmation |

## Retainer Lanes

- Site architecture and AI/search visibility.
- Product or landing page conversion.
- Search trust cleanup and real off-site trust/distribution.
- Email/SMS campaigns.
- Ad angle and creative concepts.
- Competitor watch.
- Analytics and decision support.

## Renewal Moment

At the end of each month, show:

- what changed
- what shipped
- what was learned
- what remains highest leverage
- what next month will focus on

Do not pitch renewal, expansion, or a higher retainer unless the weekly report shows shipped work, a learning, a next test, and client confirmation that the value was seen, understood, approved for next action, and worth continuing.
