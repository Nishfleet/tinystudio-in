# Retired: Expansion Triggers

> **Status: Retired historical reference.** This multi-service expansion routine is not part of the one-page sprint. Do not use it as an active offer, delivery, or continuation path.

Do not add services randomly. Expand only when a trigger appears.

## Add Email/SMS When

- Client has a list.
- Client sells repeat purchases, appointments, renewals, or promotions.
- Existing emails are weak or irregular.
- The client can approve sends quickly.

## Add Ads/Creative When

- Client already runs ads or is ready to test.
- Offer and landing page are clear.
- Client can provide spend/result screenshots.
- Creative angles are the bottleneck.

## Add Competitor Watch When

- Client is in a crowded category.
- Competitors change offers often.
- Client struggles with positioning.
- Sales calls mention competitors often.

## Add Search Trust / Distribution When

- The conversion fix is clear but the site is hard for searchers to understand.
- Service pages lack title/meta, headings, FAQs, internal links, local relevance, or crawl basics.
- The client has real citations, partner/vendor listings, reviews, proof assets, or community mentions to collect.
- Off-site work can build trust without buying backlinks or manufacturing fake mentions.

## Add Analytics Decision Room When

- Client has enough traffic or pipeline data.
- Team argues about priorities.
- Weekly decisions are slow.
- The owner wants a single growth operating rhythm.

## Do Not Expand When

- The current lane is not stable.
- The client is not approving or shipping.
- There is no measurement signal.
- The new lane is only being added to justify a higher invoice.
- The proposed off-site work is backlinks, fake reviews, spam directories, or a ranking promise.
