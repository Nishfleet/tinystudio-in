# Outsized Value Retention System

This is the monthly retention product.

## North Star

Every week, the client should be able to say:

"TinyStudio found a tangible leak, shipped or handed off a fix, showed proof, and made the next decision easier."

## Weekly Value Stack

| Layer | Required Output | Why It Retains |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion fix | One page, form, CTA, proof, offer, or email improvement. | Buyers see a clearer path to action. |
| Search trust | Title/meta, headings, links, FAQs, schema where useful, crawl basics, or local relevance. | The fixed page becomes easier for searchers and AI/search systems to understand. |
| Real trust/distribution | Citation, Google Business Profile, partner/vendor listing, review, proof asset, useful citeable page, or real PR/community mention. | The client sees distribution work that compounds without risky backlink schemes. |
| Proof packet | Before, after, proof source, client-visible value, and next measurement. | The client does not have to trust vague claims. |
| Decision support | One next action, owner, source, next check, and decision rule. | The client knows what to do next and why. |
| Delight | One useful extra that reduces decision burden. | The work feels more valuable than the invoice. |

## Monthly Retention Bar

Do not ask for renewal unless the month has:

- at least two shipped or implementation-ready improvements
- at least one search trust improvement
- at least one proof/distribution asset
- a client-facing dashboard with a value proof score of 8/10 or higher
- a measurement contract for the next loop
- a client-confirmed continue/retain signal

## What Counts As Outsized Value

- The client can inspect the before/after.
- The fix is implementation-ready, not vague.
- The recommendation ties to visible evidence, analytics, customer language, or approved proof.
- The work reduces the client's decision burden.
- The next measurement is clear enough to judge next week.

## What Does Not Count

- More pages with no priority.
- More ideas with no owner.
- Strategy without implementation-ready copy or structure.
- SEO activity without a conversion fix.
- Off-site work built around bought links, fake mentions, or ranking promises.
- A dashboard with no shipped work or client-confirmed value.

## Weekly Question

Before sending any update, ask:

"Would a busy founder or operator understand why paying us again next week is useful?"

If the answer is not obvious, improve the proof packet, simplify the next action, or add one real quick win.
