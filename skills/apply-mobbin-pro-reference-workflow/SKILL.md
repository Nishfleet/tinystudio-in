# Apply Mobbin Pro Reference Workflow

## Principle

Improve the preview from fresh business truth and fresh Mobbin Pro MCP reference evidence.

The workflow should make the page more commercially credible for the specific business, not merely different from the previous screenshot.

## Required Context

- `MEMORY.md`
- `context/design-quality-pipeline.md`
- the lead or preview `brief.json`
- business reality pack or `data.json`
- current rendered preview files
- current Mobbin search brief, raw pull log, reference packet, and ingredient ledger

## Sequence

1. Confirm the business facts, source limits, photo rights, primary action, and visitor decision.
2. Confirm the Mobbin packet belongs to this business/run and has matching hashes, query log, raw pull log, 12-20 candidates, 6-8 shortlist references, and 2+ anti-references.
3. Extract or update the ingredient ledger across IA, typography, spacing, responsive behavior, color, surfaces, components, interaction, media, proof, CTAs, accessibility, performance, and governance.
4. Explore 6-8 visual territories from the taxonomy and shortlist 3-4 business-specific Mobbin-backed finalist concepts.
5. Pick one finalist, reject the others with reasons, and write the build brief.
6. Edit the preview from the build brief and structured data.
7. Capture desktop and mobile evidence.
8. Run similarity, truth, accessibility, performance, responsive, and commercial visual review.

## Evidence

Save or update:

- search brief
- raw pull log path and hash
- reference packet path and hash
- selected and rejected references
- selected and rejected ingredients
- chosen direction and rejected alternatives
- build brief
- files edited, or no-op reason
- screenshots
- commercial visual verdict: `approved`, `rejected`, or `needs another direction`

## Output

- Updated preview files.
- Updated `brief.json` or `qa-report.json` when source confidence, send status, or design notes change.
- A short summary of what changed and why the chosen direction is stronger.
- A saved evidence packet proving the workflow was followed.

## Hard Stops

- Do not invent reviews, awards, owners, prices, opening hours, certifications, or results.
- Do not make sourced-but-unapproved images look like final approved business assets.
- Do not prepare outreach if QA is `hold`.
- Do not use a stale Mobbin packet or a static reference bank as current evidence.
- Do not mark a preview sendable just because it is visually different.
- Do not redesign from code alone when no Mobbin-backed direction has been chosen.
