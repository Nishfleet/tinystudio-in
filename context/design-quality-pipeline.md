# Design Quality Pipeline

The lab's design system is the Mobbin Pro MCP reference-led workflow.

## Source Of Taste

Every serious redesign starts with the specific business:

1. define the business model, niche, customer decision, primary action, proof limits, and visual constraints
2. build a fresh Mobbin search brief for that exact job
3. pull fresh Mobbin Pro MCP website references across niche, page job, tone, and constraints
4. save the full raw pull in `raw-pull-log.json`
5. compress the raw pool into a reference packet with 12-20 candidates, 6-8 shortlisted references, and 2+ anti-references
6. extract ingredients across business job, IA, content, voice, typography, layout, spacing, responsive behavior, color, surfaces, depth, shape, components, states, interaction, motion, media, iconography, illustration, proof, CTAs, forms, accessibility, localization, SEO/AEO, performance, tokens, and governance
7. remix compatible ingredients into one coherent direction for that business

Do not copy source brands, logos, claims, screenshots, or exact layouts. Mobbin references are taste and structure evidence, not business proof.

## Breadth Rule

Do not narrow research to keep the transcript clean. Pull broadly, save locally, and report only counts, hashes, shortlist IDs, and blockers in chat.

A reference packet cannot pass unless it links to a matching raw pull log, proves every required query angle ran, and shows at least 32 returned screens and 20 unique screens before compression.

## Variability Gate

Two outputs fail if they feel like the same template with changed colors, fonts, copy, images, icons, or card order.

Strong outputs must vary in structure, typography rhythm, media treatment, CTA layout, section order, proof presentation, and interaction details while keeping the same truth, mobile, performance, and source-backed proof standards.

## Mobbin Direction Gate

Every benchmark or serious redesign needs three Mobbin-backed directions before code:

- safe: lowest-risk commercial fit
- bold: strongest differentiated fit
- weird-but-plausible: a credible stretch that could still sell the business

Each direction must name the Mobbin references used, the anti-references rejected, the ingredients selected, the ingredients rejected, why the mix belongs together, and why the direction fits that business. Reject weak directions before code.

## Build Brief

Before code, write a build brief from the selected Mobbin-backed direction:

- section order and above-fold layout
- typography role and rhythm
- color system and accent behavior
- media direction and no-photo fallback if needed
- CTA hierarchy and contact path
- proof architecture and forbidden claims
- responsive risks
- performance budget
- implementation tokens

Codex builds from the business truth, the fresh Mobbin packet, and this brief.

## Send Gate

A preview is not sendable because the packet exists. It still needs rendered desktop/mobile screenshots, similarity review against benchmark outputs, accessibility/performance/truth checks, and human commercial-strength approval.

If proof is missing, the output stays blocked.
