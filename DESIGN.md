# Design Context

## Design Register

Marketing and local-business landing pages. Design is part of the product because the preview itself is the sales proof.

## Design Direction

- Default to distinctive local editorial systems, not generic SaaS pages.
- Use each business category as a constraint: barbers need menu, hours, and direct contact; salons need service paths and real hair proof; clinics need careful claims and consultation flow; spas need packages, duration, facilities, and calm proof.
- Prefer grounded details, sourced photos, service menus, location, and booking paths over decorative abstraction.

## Typography

- Avoid Inter and system-font sameness for generated previews.
- Pair a characterful display face with a readable body face.
- Keep body copy tight, direct, and readable; headings should be balanced but not oversized enough to crowd mobile.

## Color

- Avoid purple-blue AI gradients, pure black, pure white, and one-note beige/purple/dark-slate palettes.
- Use one primary accent per preview, with supporting neutrals tinted toward the business mood.
- Use color to support hierarchy, not as proof or decoration.

## Layout

- Avoid equal repeated card grids as the default.
- Use asymmetric editorial layouts, proof-led service menus, lookbook rails, clinical ledgers, poster treatments, and contact-first structures based on the business.
- Mobile must collapse into a clean single-column path with no horizontal scroll.
- The first viewport must show the business name, service fit, location or area, and a real next action.

## Motion And Detail

- Use small, purposeful motion only: hover, press, scroll anchoring, and light staggered reveals.
- Animate transform and opacity, not layout properties.
- Respect reduced-motion.
- Use image outlines, clear focus rings, tactile buttons, and consistent hit areas.

## QA Taste Rules

- A page that is “beautiful” but could belong to any business is a failure.
- A page that is varied but factually thin is not sendable.
- Any preview using sourced-but-unapproved images or facts remains research/review, not outreach-ready.
- Internal preview caveats belong near the bottom or in QA files, never as the hero message.
